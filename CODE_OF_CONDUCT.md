# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

0xda-market is committed to a professional, respectful, and technically rigorous environment.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas, code, and decisions without personal attacks;
- give actionable feedback grounded in observable behavior and documented contracts;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives;
- keep disagreements focused on the work;
- protect private information, credentials, and security-sensitive details;
- accept maintainer decisions made to preserve project safety and scope.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of private or security-sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to organization maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not publish sensitive reports in issues or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from organization spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in organization repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where a participant represents 0xda-market.
